Henry Wiltshire Estate Agents are delighted to present No. 5 Millbank Square, a superb two-bedroom ground floor apartment ideally located in the heart of Sallins Village.

Presented in very good condition throughout, this bright and spacious home offers stylish and comfortable living in a highly convenient setting. The property will appeal to first-time buyers, downsizers, and investors seeking a well-located home close to every amenity.

The accommodation briefly comprises an entrance hallway with hot press and additional storage. This leads into a bright open-plan kitchen, dining, and living area with excellent natural light throughout. The living space features a fireplace with the option for an electric insert and opens onto a private patio overlooking a mature green area, providing an ideal space to relax outdoors. The fully fitted kitchen includes an oven, hob, extractor fan, washing machine, and fridge freezer. There are two generous double bedrooms, both with built-in wardrobes. The bathroom has been recently renovated and is finished to a modern standard.

Additional benefits include gas-fired central heating, communal parking, and well-maintained landscaped grounds.

Millbank Square enjoys an excellent location within walking distance of Sallins’ many amenities, including shops, cafés, restaurants, schools, and the Grand Canal. Sallins Train Station is just a short walk away, providing regular commuter services to Dublin City Centre. The nearby Sallins bypass also offers quick access to the N7/M7 motorway network, making commuting straightforward. Kerry Group headquarters is also located nearby.

This is an excellent opportunity to acquire a spacious and easily maintained home in one of Sallins’ most convenient residential developments. Early viewing is highly recommended.
